Planned maintenance

  • Sign-in system update

    Signing in may fail for a few minutes during the update. If you are already signed in, you stay signed in. If a code does not work, wait a moment and ask for a new one.

  • Move of the app's notification service

    Order and delivery notifications in the app may arrive up to an hour late while the service moves to new servers. The app itself and placing orders are not affected.

  • Database upgrade

    The shop may be slow or show a maintenance page for up to an hour. We chose a late Monday evening, when the fewest people shop. Orders already placed are not affected.
