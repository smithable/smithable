Order confirmation emails delayed

October 2, 2026

Edit

The service that sends order confirmations stopped after a nightly update at 02:10 and did not start again. Emails waited in a queue until the team restarted it in the morning. Orders were received and paid as normal the whole time; only the emails were late.

All waiting emails have been sent. We have added an alert for when the queue grows, and we are watching the service through the morning before we close this.
