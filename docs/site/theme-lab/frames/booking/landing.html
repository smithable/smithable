Our classes

Something for every level, from first steps to deep stretches.

  • Power flow

    A strong, quick sequence for those who know the basic postures well. Arm balances and inversions included.

    Advanced

  • Yin

    Long, quiet holds on the floor that ease the joints and the mind. Bring warm socks.

    All levels

  • Vinyasa

    Postures linked to the breath in a flowing sequence. Expect to move and to sweat a little.

    All levels

  • Hatha

    Slow, steady postures held for a few breaths each. You leave stretched, warm and rested.

    All levels

  • Foundations

    The basic postures, one at a time, with time to ask questions. A good first class if you have never done yoga.

    Beginner
