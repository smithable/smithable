About us

A small studio by the harbour

Stillwater began in 2019 as one evening class a week in a borrowed room above a bakery. Today it is a studio of its own on Storagatan, a few minutes from the harbour, with classes from seven in the morning to late evening.

The classes stay small, never more than sixteen mats, so every teacher knows your name and how your practice is going. The room has wooden floors, tall windows and a radiator under each of them, which matters in February.

We keep things simple. You book a mat online, you cancel it if plans change, and if you want to talk about your practice, there is time for that after class.

Questions before you come

Do I need experience?

No. Foundations and all-levels classes are made for beginners, and every posture has an easier version.

How early should I arrive?

Ten minutes before the class, to settle in and find your mat. We close the door when the class starts, to keep the room quiet.

Which languages do the teachers speak?

All classes are taught in Swedish and English. Aino also teaches in Finnish if you ask.

Is there a changing room?

Yes, with lockers and two showers. Bring a padlock or borrow one at the desk.

Can I buy a gift card?

Yes, for one class or for a month. Ask at the desk or write to us, and we will post it or keep it for you to collect.
