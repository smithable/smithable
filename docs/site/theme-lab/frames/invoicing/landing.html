Good to know

Questions

Can I use my own invoice numbers?

Yes. Numbers are yours to choose. Most people count per year, like 2026-001, 2026-002.

Who can see my invoices?

Only you. Each freelancer sees their own clients and invoices, and nobody else's.

Can I change an invoice after sending it?

A sent or paid invoice is not edited; you credit it instead. A draft you can change as often as you like.

What happens when a client pays late?

A week after the due date a sent invoice is marked overdue, and the overview shows how much is late in total.

Can I start a new invoice from an old one?

Yes. Duplicate an invoice and its lines come along. Give the copy its own number and change what differs.
