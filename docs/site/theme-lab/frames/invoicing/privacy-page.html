Privacy

This page says what Invoicely stores about you and why. It is written from the app's own definition and changes with it.

Who runs it

Invoicely is run by Invoicely Ab.

Torggatan 5, 22100 Mariehamn, Åland, Finland · hello@invoicely.example

Invoicely: Simple invoicing for freelancers. Each user manages their own clients and invoices.

Your account

When you sign in, the app keeps your name, your email address and your role, and sets a cookie that keeps you signed in. You sign in with a code sent to your email address.

Information in records

Besides accounts, the app keeps these kinds of records with personal details:

  • Clients: email. Read by: admin.

Pictures and documents people upload are stored with their records and shown to those who may read the record.

Cookies

The app sets one cookie, which keeps you signed in. It is needed for the app to work. There is no tracking cookie.

Mail

The app sends mail for signing in and for invitations.

It also sends mail when something happens to invoices, to the person concerned or to the people who work in the app.

Logs and backups

Requests are logged with your address and browser for 90 days. Changes to records are kept in an activity log for 12 months; names and addresses in it are removed after 90 days.

Backups are kept so the app can be restored after a mistake.

Your rights

You may ask what the app holds about you, have it corrected or removed, and object to its use. Write to hello@invoicely.example. You may also complain to the data protection authority in your country.
